## 🚀 About SmartBiz AI

SmartBiz AI is a modern SaaS platform designed to help businesses automate customer engagement through WhatsApp.

Instead of manually replying to messages, scheduling appointments, and tracking leads, SmartBiz AI acts as a virtual sales representative available 24 hours a day.

### What SmartBiz AI Can Do

✅ Respond instantly to customer inquiries

✅ Capture and qualify leads automatically

✅ Schedule appointments without human intervention

✅ Track conversations and customer history

✅ Provide real-time business analytics

✅ Generate AI-powered daily performance reports

✅ Support both Arabic and English customers
